01The problem

You already know the hard part.

The central problems of securing AI agents have statistical twins. The course supplies the security half.

01

Rare events

Attacks are rare. How often an alert is right is set by the base rate, not by how good the detector looks on a balanced test set.

02

Operating points

Every threshold spends a false-alarm budget. You choose where to spend it, and measure it where the system acts.

03

Distribution shift

A detector tested on prose meets JSON, logs, diffs and SQL in production, and behaves differently.

04

Counterfactuals

Will a new policy help? Replay it over last month's traffic and run it in shadow before you enforce it.

  6. M07A red-team harness with honest coverage reports

  8. M09Behavioural baselines and sequence detectors

  10. M11Personal-data detection, redaction and erasure
  11. M12A signed, tamper-evident log, and replay of a policy change
  12. M13A policy learner with a shadow stage
04Scale

Build it small. Reason about it at scale.

Every module ends with what the same component becomes at millions of calls a day, thousands of agents and many teams, worked out from your own measurements.

On your laptop
40ms

Your detector scores one tool call in 40 milliseconds. You measured it, you understand every line, and it runs in 8 GB of memory with no GPU.

At 50 million calls a day
23cores

The same detector keeps about 23 cores busy around the clock. Where does it run? What happens to a call when it is slow or down? Who reviews its alerts?

07Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need a security background?

No. The course assumes Python, probability and machine learning, and teaches the security from the ground up.

What do I need to run it?

A laptop with 8 GB of memory, running macOS, Windows (with WSL 2) or Linux. No GPU is required. Installing Docker and setting up the lab is a module of its own.

Which products does it teach?

None. You build every component yourself with open, general-purpose tools, and wherever a tool is chosen the lesson names an alternative.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every lesson, build and automated check is free. Only the final exam, which a person reviews, and the certificate that follows are paid.

In which languages?

English and Hebrew. Code, commands and tools stay in English in both.

When does it open?

It is in preparation. Join the waitlist to hear when the first modules are published.
