BuildingAI agents

For data scientists and engineers building with AI. Understand each part of an agent, from the model to production, and learn to evaluate its performance. Start with the basics and work through to current research. Study before or alongside Defending AI Agents.

Thirteen modules, from the model to production.

About 101 hours in total, from the basics to current research.

  1. 01LLM mechanics for builders
  2. 02Prompting and context engineering
  3. 03The agent loop, from scratch
  4. 04Frameworks and SDKs, and what they hide
  5. 05Retrieval and memory
  6. 06Protocols: MCP, A2A and tools from OpenAPI
  7. 07Orchestration and multi-agent systems
  8. 08Harnesses: coding, browser and computer-use agents
  9. 09Evaluating agents for quality
  10. 10Serving and inference
  11. 11LLMOps and observability
  12. 12Deployment and platform basics

02How it works

Build it. Measure how it performs.

Each module ends with practical measurements using local models on your own laptop. Make a change, measure the results and see how it affects your agent’s performance.

03With Defending AI Agents

Building AI Agents lays the engineering foundations. Defending AI Agents adds attacks, defences, evaluation and incident response. You can study them in sequence or alongside each other.
