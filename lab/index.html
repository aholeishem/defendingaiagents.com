Free lab · 60 minutes · no installs

The assistant thatgave the secret away

An AI assistant reads documents and sends e-mail. One document comes from a supplier's portal, and the attacker controls the portal. Make the assistant leak a confidential file, write two defences, and measure what each one stops and what it breaks.

Time60 minNeedsPython 3.10+InstallsnoneLanguageEN · HE

Get the lab What you will do →

01What you will do

Four parts, one hour.

  1. 01Attack. Write a supplier document that makes the assistant send a confidential file to an outside address. Try other wordings until it works.
  2. 02A first defence. Write the defence most people write first, a word filter, and run the checks: attacks that still get through, legitimate jobs that break.
  3. 03A structural defence. Ask where the destination address came from, and enforce the answer in the e-mail tool itself.
  4. 04What you measured. Zero leaks in seven tries is not proof. Work out what the result does and does not tell you, and what it means at 100,000 e-mails a day.
02Compare your results

What the four configurations score.

Seven attacks and five legitimate jobs, on a simulated model with fixed behaviour, so that every difference comes from the defence. Run the lab and see whether yours matches.
